Dice Quest

Roll the Roversa die five times to get five gamepieces. Put them in order and press GO. First robot to visit the flag, the chest and the gem wins!

Every rolled piece must be used. The green Roversa logo piece is wild: it can be any arrow. The red Roversa word piece repeats the piece before it. If Roversa drives off the map it goes back to the start.
